# Changelog

What changed in each version of PicViewer, newest first. Every release is on the [releases page](../../releases) with the app to download.

## Unreleased

The first version.

- Open a picture and step through its folder with ← and →, in the same order as Finder shows it: list view sorting, icon view Sort By or icon positions, or the view settings saved for the folder.
- ⌘R and ⇧⌘R rotate the picture in the file itself, without losing any quality and without a save step.
- Settings… (⌘,) can rotate JPEGs by turning the pixels instead of changing the orientation tag, for apps and websites that ignore the tag. It's just as lossless.
- ⌘⌫ moves the picture to the Trash, and ⌘Z puts it back.
- The info sidebar (⌘I) shows the file, the camera, lens and exposure settings.
